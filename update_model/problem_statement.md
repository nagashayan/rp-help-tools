When a visually impaired person is in social gathering or meeting a person, when the opposite person extends their hand to initiate handshake
and the visually impaired person doesn't see it because the hand of the other person is below their peripheral vision level or it is in one of
their blind spots.

This is a common problem in people with Inherited retinal problem.

**how did I find this is common**

I know people with Retinitis Pigmentosa and Glaucoma patients face this social anxiety because I am part of their social groups on social media.

Solution?

Detect the handshake initiation and give a cue to the visually impaired person so they know the opposite person is initiating an handshake towards them and can
raise their hands for handshake and avoid embarrasment and anxiety of missing handshakes from prior experience.

This could be extended to different social interactions like fist bump, high fi etc
