
# Gesture Recognition with Context: Papers and Datasets

A collection of notable papers in gesture recognition with contextual understanding, ordered by journal prominence and citation count, along with the datasets used.

---

## 1. "Temporal Context Modeling for Gesture Recognition Using 3D Convolutional Neural Networks"
- **Journal**: IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI) – One of the highest impact journals in computer vision.
- **Dataset**: **ChaLearn LAP IsoGD Dataset** and **Jester Dataset**. The ChaLearn dataset includes over 47,000 video samples, while Jester has around 150,000 labeled clips of human gestures.

---

## 2. "Contextual-Based Gesture Recognition Using Deep Learning Models"
- **Journal**: IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems
- **Dataset**: **MSRC-12 Gesture Dataset** and **NTU RGB+D Dataset**. The MSRC-12 dataset includes 12 gestures, and the NTU RGB+D dataset contains 56,880 samples with depth data.

---

## 3. "Learning Contextual Relationships for Gesture Recognition"
- **Journal**: Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR)
- **Dataset**: **HCI Gesture Dataset** and **UCF-101**. The HCI dataset focuses on human-computer interaction research, while UCF-101 is used for action context due to diverse scenes and actions.

---

## 4. "Environmentally Adaptive Gesture Recognition Using Transfer Learning and Context Awareness"
- **Journal**: IEEE Transactions on Image Processing
- **Dataset**: **EgoGesture Dataset** and **Kinetics-400**. EgoGesture has first-person perspective videos, and Kinetics-400 supports transfer learning with 400 action classes.

---

## 5. "Gesture Recognition in Complex Environments Using Multi-modal Contextual Cues"
- **Journal**: IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters
- **Dataset**: **GMU-Kinect Dataset** and **Berkeley MHAD**. The GMU-Kinect dataset includes multi-modal data, and Berkeley MHAD includes motion capture data for multimodal modeling.

---

## 6. "Contextual Augmented Gesture Recognition for Smart Healthcare Systems"
- **Journal**: IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics
- **Dataset**: **Custom Healthcare Dataset**. The dataset was created specifically for healthcare scenarios, capturing patient gestures with cameras and sensors.

---

## 7. "A Context-Aware Approach to Hand Gesture Recognition in Human-Robot Interaction"
- **Journal**: International Journal of Social Robotics
- **Dataset**: **Cambridge Hand Gesture Dataset** and **Custom HRI Dataset**. The Cambridge dataset includes hand gestures for HRI, and the custom dataset focuses on social robotics contexts.

---

## 8. "Dynamic Gesture Recognition with Contextual Awareness for Driver Assistance Systems"
- **Journal**: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems
- **Dataset**: **Drive&Act Dataset** and **Custom Automotive Dataset**. Drive&Act is aimed at in-vehicle activities, and the custom dataset includes driver-specific gestures.

---

## 9. "Context-Aware Gesture Recognition in Multi-User Environments"
- **Journal**: IEEE Access
- **Dataset**: **Montalbano Gesture Dataset** and **Custom Multi-User Dataset**. Montalbano features multi-user gestures, while the custom dataset includes crowded scenarios with labeled gestures.

---

## 10. "Integrating Contextual Memory for Gesture Recognition in Wearable Devices"
- **Journal**: Sensors
- **Dataset**: **SHREC 2017 Dataset** and **Ninapro DB5**. SHREC is used in wearable tech research, and Ninapro DB5 includes electromyography data for hand gestures.

---
